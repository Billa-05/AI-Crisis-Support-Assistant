AI Crisis Support Assistant

A lightweight and supportive AI tool designed to provide calm, safe, non-medical guidance during moments of emotional distress such as anxiety, panic, overwhelm, or loneliness.

This assistant does not replace professional help. It simply offers grounding techniques, breathing guidance, and supportive messages — especially helpful for immigrants, students, and people under daily stress.

⭐ Features

🧘 Calming grounding exercises

🌬 Breathing support messages

💬 Reassuring, empathetic responses

⚠️ Safe emergency guidance (call emergency services if unsafe)

🤖 Powered by an AI language model

🪶 Simple, clean, beginner-friendly code

🧠 How It Works

The user types how they feel.
The AI analyzes the emotional tone and responds with:

grounding steps

breathing guidance

comforting messages

reminders to stay safe

▶️ Example Usage
User: I feel scared and I can’t breathe properly.
AI: You're safe. Let's slow down together. 
Try this: Inhale for 4 seconds… hold for 2… exhale for 6.
You’re not alone. If you feel physically unsafe, please contact local emergency services immediately.
